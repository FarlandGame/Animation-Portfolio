## Creator's account and reading notes

An in-game Minecraft retelling of Mush-o Gorbeh (Mouse and Cat), traditionally attributed to Obeyd-e Zakani (عبید زاکانی), identified by me as the story source. I used a spectator / replay-like camera method, but I do not confidently identify a specific Replay Mod workflow. I supplied the voice work and Persian text, while my brother Armiya and I played the characters. This is Minecraft capture, not a Blender-rendered film. Its complete 1080p viewing copy is recompressed at my request.

[Mush-o Gorbeh / موش و گربه](https://ganjoor.net/obeyd/moosh-gorbe/) · [Obeyd-e Zakani — Encyclopaedia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/obayd-zakani/)
