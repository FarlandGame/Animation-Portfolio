## Creator's account and reading notes

A short biographical school project about Adolf Hitler, assembled from animation-era material, images, footage and English on-screen text. I performed the girl's narration myself, altering my voice. I remember the mixed-media approach as a substantial step beyond my earlier exercises. The archive preserves an old school project; it does not independently fact-check its historical narration or claim that all included footage/images were created from scratch by me.
