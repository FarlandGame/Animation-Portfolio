## Creator's account and reading notes

My most ambitious surviving StickNodes-era project: a mathematics story about coordinates, made for a Khwarizmi competition. I recall more than 13,000 animation frames, about one month of construction and several days of editing; those are my recollections, not an independently counted drawing total. The supplied film is nearly thirteen minutes long and includes coordinate diagrams, character scenes and some English on-screen captions. It was not submitted before the deadline and is not a prize-winning competition entry. The full viewing copy is recompressed at my request; no part of its running time is intentionally removed.
