## Creator's account and reading notes

My earliest surviving export in the numbered StickNodes-era group: a short street scene made on my phone. I recall being around 12–13 and feeling proud of it then. Today I keep it as an early experiment, with rough timing and simple staging visible. It belongs to seventh grade, not my present production standard.
