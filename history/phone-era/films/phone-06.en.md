## Creator's account and reading notes

A fictional cautionary school story about a character called Amir Hossein Houri who runs away from school, turns to stealing, is caught and receives a fictional three-year prison sentence. This is a story synopsis, not an allegation about a real person. I voiced multiple characters and used Persian on-screen text. I remember the longer film being well received at school during ninth grade and helping classmates notice my work. A second part was planned and partly made, but no surviving export is supplied.
