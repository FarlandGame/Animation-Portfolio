## Creator's account and reading notes

An early Minecraft server recording that I originally thought of as a kind of 3D animation. Today I identify it more precisely as gameplay / in-game storytelling. I built the server and its contraptions; my twin brother Armiya played Herobrine while I tried to escape. The supplied file preserves the game's mobile interface and recording overlays.
