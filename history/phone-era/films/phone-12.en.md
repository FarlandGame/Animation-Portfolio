## Creator's account and reading notes

The numbering exception and the earliest surviving work in this batch: a short school anti-smoking video recorded inside a game. I identify it with Dey 1401, seventh grade, shortly after receiving my first phone, for Farhang Shahid Parkar School in Maragheh. It contains Persian text and my younger voice with effects. It predates number 1 despite being supplied as 12.mp4. The complete 1080p viewing copy is recompressed at my request.
