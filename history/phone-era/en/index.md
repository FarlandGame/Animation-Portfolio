These are the surviving exports from my early phone-based creative years: StickNodes-era animation, a mixed-media school biography, Minecraft storytelling, my first original mobile 3D scene and an early channel opening. I have grown beyond their rough edges, but keeping them makes the learning visible. They are historical work, not a showcase of my current production quality.

> My first frames had big confidence. The keyframes were still catching up. 📱😄

## How to read the timeline

I remember making numbers 1–4 in seventh grade, during the 1401–1402 Solar Hijri school year, and numbers 5–8 in ninth grade. Number 12 is the exception to numerical order: I identify it as older than number 1, from Dey 1401, shortly after getting my first phone. Other exact production dates are not established. Number 13 is a separate early branding opening, not the newest animation by quality or chronology.

## Voices, software and collaboration

I performed the Persian voices, often changing them with effects. Number 5 teaches a ninth-grade Work and Technology lesson; number 6 uses several character voices; number 7 includes my performance of a girl's narration. English on-screen text is visible in parts of 7 and 8, but this archive does not claim complete subtitles in 18 languages. My twin brother Armiya plays Herobrine in 9 and joins the characters in 11.

## School-location reference gallery

The 46 photographs are real school-location references I took for an unfinished second school-escape story. They show Farhang Shahid Parkar School, Maragheh, as I identify it. I photographed the locations during one visit; these images are background-planning material, not completed animation frames. The original files are available together in a source pack, with smaller full-frame gallery previews.

## Projects that did not reach the archive

Some earlier work, my eighth-grade experiments and part of the planned school-story sequel were not retained. They cannot be included or reconstructed here. The coordinates project was made for a Khwarizmi competition, but it was not submitted before the deadline; it is not presented as a competition entry or award. These exports belong to the path that led toward my book and later 3D work.
